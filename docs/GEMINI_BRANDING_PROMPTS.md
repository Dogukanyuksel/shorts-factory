# Gemini Marka, İsim, Logo ve Banner Prompt Paketi (2 Kanal)

Aşağıdaki promptları doğrudan **Gemini**'ye kopyalayıp yapıştırarak hem alternatif telifsiz İngilizce kanal isimleri hem de istediğin tarzda yeni **Logo (1:1)** ve **YouTube Banner (16:9)** görselleri üretebilirsin.

---

## 1. Telifsiz İngilizce İsim & Handle Önerisi İsteme Promptu (Gemini Sohbet)

```text
I am launching two separate English-language YouTube Shorts channels targeting US & global audiences:
1) Channel 1: 60fps deterministic physics, math, and algorithm simulations with short scientific voiceover commentary (marble gauntlets, pendulum waves, sorting algorithm races, elastic collisions, exponential growth).
2) Channel 2: 60-second animated engineering & science explainers ("How It Actually Works" — pin tumbler locks, airplane wings, noise-canceling headphones, car differentials, submarines, QR codes).

Give me 10 original, trademark-safe, ultra-premium English brand name ideas + matching @handles + 2-sentence YouTube bios for EACH channel. Avoid generic clickbait words and avoid existing big channel names. Keep names short (2 words max), memorable, and authoritative like Veritasium or 3Blue1Brown.
```

---

## 2. Kanal 1 (Kinetic Lab / Fizik & Bilim) — Görsel Promptları

### Logo Promptu (1:1 Kare — İkon + İsim Görünür)
```text
Create an ultra-premium modern science YouTube channel profile logo (1:1 square) on a deep obsidian dark navy background (#06080f). Center a sleek geometric prism 'K' emblem made of frosted titanium-white and glowing electric cyan (#38bdf8) light vectors, with bold, razor-sharp white geometric sans-serif text 'KINETIC LAB' directly underneath and a thin glowing cyan accent line. Keep generous dark padding around all edges so the entire icon and text fit cleanly inside YouTube's circular profile crop. High contrast, Apple-grade minimalism, NO rings, studio quality.
```

### Banner Promptu (16:9 Yatay — YouTube Safe Zone Uyumlu)
```text
Create a sleek modern YouTube channel banner (16:9 widescreen, high resolution) on a deep dark navy background (#06080f) with subtle faint blueprint grid lines and soft cyan/indigo physics trajectory curves near the outer edges. Strictly centered in the middle safe zone (middle 50% width and middle 35% height so it displays cleanly on mobile, desktop, and TV): bold crisp white geometric sans-serif title 'KINETIC LAB', a glowing cyan divider line, and clean subtitle 'PHYSICS • MATH • 60FPS SIMULATIONS' below it. Generous empty dark margin on all four sides. Ultra-clean tech-science aesthetic.
```

## 3. Kanal 2 (Clear Mechanics / Nasıl Çalışır) — Görsel Promptları

### Logo Promptu (1:1 Kare — İkon + İsim Görünür)
```text
Create a clean, ultra-premium engineering YouTube channel profile logo (1:1 square) on a deep blueprint slate-navy background (#0b132b). Center a sharp minimalist mechanical cutaway gear emblem in crisp white, electric cyan (#38bdf8), and a warm amber (#fbbf24) core, with clean bold white geometric sans-serif text 'CLEAR MECHANICS' stacked neatly underneath. Keep generous dark padding around all sides so both the emblem and text stay completely visible inside YouTube's circular avatar crop. Flat modern vector-inspired style, studio quality.
```

### Banner Promptu (16:9 Yatay — YouTube Safe Zone Uyumlu)
```text
Create a modern educational engineering YouTube channel banner (16:9 widescreen, high resolution) on a deep blueprint slate-navy background (#0b132b) with subtle faint white technical schematic drafting lines and gear cross-sections fading softly toward the edges. Strictly centered in the middle safe zone (middle 50% width and middle 35% height for mobile + desktop compatibility): bold crisp white sans-serif title 'CLEAR MECHANICS', a warm amber (#fbbf24) accent bar, and clean subtitle 'HOW THINGS ACTUALLY WORK • ENGINEERING IN 60 SECONDS'. Generous dark breathing room around all edges.
```

## 4. YouTube Ölçü İpucu
- **Profil Logosu:** Minimum `800 × 800 px` (1:1 kare, YouTube yuvarlak kırpar — metin ve ikon ortada olmalı).
- **Kanal Bannerı:** `2560 × 1440 px` (16:9 yatay, dosya boyutu 6 MB altı; yazı mutlaka tam ortada olmalı ki mobilde kesilmesin).
